# Photo shopping list

The files in this folder are generated placeholders. Drop a real photo in with
the **same filename** and it appears everywhere that slot is used — no markup
change needed. Match the pixel size (or the aspect ratio, at least as large).

Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash and StockSnap are all fine for commercial use without
attribution; check the licence on the individual photo before you ship it.

## A note on the photos themselves

The whole pitch on these pages is "real photos, never stock". Stock imagery is
right for a placeholder and wrong for the finished site — replace these with
photographs of actual DM Tradies clients before launch, or the copy and the
pictures contradict each other.
