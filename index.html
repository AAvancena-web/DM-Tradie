Your first 90 days

What happens in the first 90 days.

  1. Days 1–30

    Audit and diagnose

    We review your technical health, your content, your links, your local signals, and your AI visibility. So we find where you are losing ground and which wins come fastest. As a result, you get a clear plan before any work starts.

  2. Days 31–60

    Fix and build

    We clear the blockers first: indexing, speed, weak pages, missing schema, broken redirects. Then we build or improve the pages most likely to bring enquiries, and start the local work.

  3. Days 61–90

    Grow and report

    We earn authority, sharpen the content, and improve your AI visibility. As a result, the first enquiries usually come in. You then get a report that ties the work to results, with the next steps set out.

Questions

The bits people always ask.

How long does a new site take?

Four to six weeks for most trade sites, from kick-off to going live. The slow part is almost always content and photos rather than build time, so the sooner we can get on site to shoot, the sooner you launch.

Do I actually own the website?

Yes. The domain, the site and every ad account are registered in your name and stay that way. There is no lock-in contract, and nothing of yours is held back if you ever decide to leave.

What does it cost?

We quote a fixed price up front, so there are no surprise invoices. What it comes to depends on how many pages you need and which services you take — you will see the number before any work starts.

Do you only work with tradies in Melbourne?

No. We are based in Melbourne but work with clients across Australia, New Zealand and the UK. Photography is the only part that needs us on site.

Can you just do the marketing on my existing site?

Yes. Take one piece or the lot. If your current site is holding the marketing back we will tell you that plainly rather than spend your budget sending traffic to pages that will not convert.

What happens after the site goes live?

You get hosting, updates and monitoring, plus a monthly report that ties the work to enquiries rather than to impressions. If you are running ads or SEO with us, that is where we set the next quarter’s priorities.
