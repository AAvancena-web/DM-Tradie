FAQs

Good questions, straight answers

Can't see what you're after? Give us a call — we're happy to talk it through, no pressure and no pitch.

How much does a trade website cost?
Most of our trade websites land between $3,500 and $9,000 depending on how many services and service areas you need. You get the full price in writing before we start, and it doesn't move unless you change the scope. Ongoing SEO, ads and care plans are billed monthly with no lock-in.
How long until my site is live?
Four weeks is typical from kickoff to launch, assuming we get your content and sign-offs back promptly. Bigger builds with booking systems or lots of service pages can run six to eight. We give you a dated schedule at the quote stage so you know exactly where things are up to.
Do I actually own the website?
Yes — completely. Your domain, hosting, website files, Google accounts and ad accounts are all registered in your name from day one, and we hand over every login at launch. If you ever decide to leave, you take the lot with you. No exit fees, no hostage situations.
How long before SEO starts working?
Local rankings usually start shifting around the 8 to 12 week mark, with the bigger gains from month four onwards. Google Ads is the opposite — that can be producing leads within a week. Most tradies we work with run both: ads for cashflow now, SEO for the long game.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. Website builds are a one-off fixed price, and our SEO, ads and care plans are month to month. We'd rather keep your business by earning it. If you want to pause or stop, 30 days' notice is all we ask.
Do you only work with tradies?
Under the DM Tradies banner, yes — plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, painters, concreters, landscapers and inspectors. That focus is the point: we already know what your customers search for. Our parent company, Digital Movement, handles everything outside the trades.
What if I already have a website?
Plenty of our clients do. Sometimes it just needs a rebuild of the pages that matter and a proper SEO setup rather than starting over. Send us the URL and we'll tell you honestly which way we'd go — including if the answer is "keep what you've got".
