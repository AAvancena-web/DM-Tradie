About DM Tradies

A local team that treats your place like our own.

DM Tradies started with one ute, one apprentice and a simple idea: show up when you say you will, quote honestly, and leave the site cleaner than you found it. Fifteen years on, we're a team of 24 licensed tradies servicing homes, agencies and businesses right across Sydney.

    Plumber, sparky, chippie and roofer on the same team — one point of contact and one invoice for the whole job.

FAQs

Good questions, straight answers

Can't see what you're after? Give us a call — we're happy to talk it through, no pressure.

How much do you charge for a callout?
Standard callouts start at $89 including the first 30 minutes on site, and we always confirm the full price with you before any work begins. If you go ahead with the job, the callout fee is credited off your invoice.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Every tradie on our team holds a current trade licence for their discipline, and DM Tradies carries $20 million public liability cover. Licence numbers and certificates of currency are available on request — just ask when we quote.
How quickly can someone get out to me?
Most non-urgent jobs are booked within 48 hours, and 98% of our customers are seen the same week. For emergencies — burst pipes, no power, storm damage — we run a 24/7 line and reach most Sydney suburbs within the hour.
Which suburbs do you service?
We cover Sydney metro and surrounds, including the Inner West, Eastern Suburbs, North Shore, Northern Beaches, Hills District, Western Sydney, Sutherland Shire and Parramatta. Not sure if you're in range? Send us your postcode and we'll confirm.
Do you offer fixed-price quotes?
On every job we can scope properly, yes. You'll get a written, itemised quote with a fixed price before we start, and it doesn't change unless you ask us to change the scope. For emergency work we quote hourly at a clearly stated rate.
What warranty do you provide?
All of our workmanship is backed by a 10-year guarantee, and manufacturer warranties apply to any parts or appliances we supply. If something isn't right, call us and we'll come back and sort it.
Do you handle insurance and strata work?
We do. We work regularly with insurers, strata managers and real estate agencies, and we can supply photo reports, scopes of works and compliance documentation to support a claim or a committee approval.
