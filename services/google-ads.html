Ads are a tap, not a strategy

SEO builds an asset slowly. Ads buy you the phone ringing this week. Used properly they fill the gaps: a quiet fortnight, a new van, a service you want more of. Used badly they are a standing order to Google for clicks from people who were never going to book.

The money is in what you exclude

Most wasted trade ad spend goes on searches that look right and are not: people after a DIY fix, students writing assignments, other tradies pricing a job, suburbs you will not drive to. Negative keywords and tight geography do more for your cost per lead than any amount of bid tinkering.

And in where they land

Sending paid traffic to your home page wastes it. Each campaign gets a page built for that search, with the matching service, the matching suburb, and one obvious next step.

What’s included

  • Search campaigns targeted to the jobs you actually want
  • Google Local Services — the badge at the top of the results
  • Landing pages built for the campaign, not just your home page
  • Negative keywords, so you stop paying for tyre-kickers
  • Call tracking and recording, so leads are counted honestly
  • Weekly eyes on spend, not a set-and-forget account

Best for: Tradies who need the phone to ring this month, not in six.

Questions

About this service.

What should I budget for Google Ads?

It depends on your trade, your area and what a job is worth to you. We work backwards from a cost per lead you are happy with rather than starting from a number we made up.

What is the difference between Search and Local Services ads?

Local Services ads sit above everything with a Google Guaranteed badge and charge per lead rather than per click. Search ads give you far more control over wording and landing pages. Most trades do best running both.

Can I pause the ads when I am busy?

Yes, and you should. That is the whole point of the channel. We turn them down when the diary fills and back up when it thins out.
