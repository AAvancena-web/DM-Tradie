Your van is your biggest billboard

It sits in a driveway on a quiet street for six hours while neighbours walk past it. Whether that is worth anything depends entirely on whether the name and the number can be read from across the road, and whether the thing looks like a business people would trust in their house.

Consistency is most of the job

The logo matters less than whether the same logo, the same colours and the same phone number appear on the van, the shirt, the quote, the invoice, the site and the Google profile. Recognition is repetition. Most trade branding fails because it was redrawn slightly differently every time someone needed it.

Built for how it gets used

We supply the artwork already laid out for your actual vehicles, already sized for the signwriter and the embroiderer, and in every file format you will be asked for. No going back to a designer every time you buy a new ute.

What’s included

  • Logo and full brand kit, in every format you will ever need
  • Vehicle livery artwork, laid out for your actual vehicles
  • Uniform, signage and business card artwork
  • Quote and invoice templates that match
  • A simple guide so it stays consistent when someone else uses it
  • All source files handed over, yours to keep

Best for: Anyone whose branding was drawn up in a hurry and never revisited.

Questions

About this service.

Can you rebrand without me losing the name people know?

Usually that is exactly the right move. We keep the equity you have built — the name, often the colour — and fix the execution around it.

Do you deal with the signwriter directly?

Yes, if you want us to. We supply print-ready artwork to their specification so nothing gets redrawn or approximated.

Do I get the source files?

Yes. Every source file, in your name. You are never locked into us to get a business card printed.
