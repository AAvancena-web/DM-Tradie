How long does a new site take?

Four to six weeks for most trade sites, from kick-off to going live. The slow part is almost always content and photos rather than build time, so the sooner we can get on site to shoot, the sooner you launch.

Do I actually own the website?

Yes. The domain, the site and every ad account are registered in your name and stay that way. There is no lock-in contract, and nothing of yours is held back if you ever decide to leave.

What does it cost?

We quote a fixed price up front, so there are no surprise invoices. What it comes to depends on how many pages you need and which services you take — you will see the number before any work starts.

Do you only work with tradies in Melbourne?

No. We are based in Melbourne but work with clients across Australia, New Zealand and the UK. Photography is the only part that needs us on site.

Can you just do the marketing on my existing site?

Yes. Take one piece or the lot. If your current site is holding the marketing back we will tell you that plainly rather than spend your budget sending traffic to pages that will not convert.

What happens after the site goes live?

You get hosting, updates and monitoring, plus a monthly report that ties the work to enquiries rather than to impressions. If you are running ads or SEO with us, that is where we set the next quarter’s priorities.

Working together

The practical stuff.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. There is no lock-in period on anything we do. Ongoing work such as hosting, SEO or ad management is month to month, and you can stop with a month’s notice.

Who writes the content?

We do, from a conversation with you. You know the work and the questions customers ask; we know what a service page needs to rank and convert. You approve every word before it goes live.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. You get a simple editor for prices, services, staff and gallery images, plus a short walkthrough on handover. Anything structural, we handle.

What if I already have a website I like?

Then we leave it alone. If the marketing is being held back by the site we will say so plainly, with the specific pages and reasons, rather than quietly spending your budget sending traffic somewhere that will not convert.

How quickly will I see results?

Ads can produce calls in the first week. SEO is slower — the first movement is usually in the second month, with the first real enquiries around month three. We will not promise you a ranking or a number, because nobody can.

Do you work with more than one tradie in the same suburb?

Not in the same trade. If we are already running local SEO for a plumber in your area we will tell you up front rather than take you both on and compete with ourselves.

No call centre and no chatbot. You get the person who would actually be working on your site, and a straight answer even when it is not the one that wins us the job.
